# Privacy policy: Curly Braces

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Curly Braces does not collect, store, sell or share your data.

**What it reads.** To format JSON, the extension reads the text of the pages you visit and of the text you paste into its editor. This happens on your device only.

**What it stores.** Your settings (switches, view style and sites you excluded) are saved with the browser's extension storage, which your browser may sync between your own signed-in devices. The text in the editor is kept only for the open tab session.

**What it sends.** Nothing. The extension makes no network requests, has no server, and uses no analytics, advertising, tracking or error-reporting services. All code is included in the package and no code is loaded from elsewhere.

**Permissions.** `storage` saves settings. `contextMenus` adds the right-click items. `activeTab` reads the current site name when you open the popup. The content script runs on pages so it can find and format JSON; it does not send what it reads anywhere.

**Children.** The extension is a developer tool and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

**Changes.** If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.

**Contact.** Open an issue at https://github.com/abhipatel2005/jsonviewer
